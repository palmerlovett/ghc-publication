Guy Hopkins Construction Co., Inc. was established in 1984 as a commercial general contractor. Diversification into site development, municipal construction and heavy concrete work define the company's evolutionary history. Its successful experience in multiple types of construction enables the company to provide owners with a broad base of solutions to meet the construction project requirements.
