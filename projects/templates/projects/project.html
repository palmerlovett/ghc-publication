Project Description

Scope of project included demolition of an existing airplane hangar and construction of a new cargo building comprising 42.000 sf with a 165'-wide clear-span structure, 210' long with 38' eve height. Cargo area included high bay lighting, dry pipe fire protection system, wall mounted louvers and exhaust fans.

  • Owner: City of Baton Rouge / EBR Parish
  • Architect/Engineer: Go Tech
  • Project Value: $2,410,000
  • Start Date: 11/01/2004
  • Completion Date: 05/01/2005
